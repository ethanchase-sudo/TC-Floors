In the details

Pattern,
texture, line.

Every material asks for something different. These are the details we obsess over.

i.

Chevron Oak

Mitred points that meet dead-center, row after row, down the length of the room.

ii.

Hexagon Tile

Even grout joints wrapping cleanly around fixtures, toilets and tub aprons.

iii.

Glazed Backsplash

Handmade variation set with care, so the color and sheen read as intended.

iv.

Shower Surrounds

Patterned feature walls, niches and floors that slope true to the drain.

v.

Wide Plank

Long boards staggered for a natural, uninterrupted run through open plans.

vi.

Large-Format Porcelain

Flat, lippage-free surfaces with tight joints and a mirror-like finish.
